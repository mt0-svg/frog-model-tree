The frog model on the rooted 4-ary tree is transient: almost surely the root is visited finitely often, and the mean number of visits is at most 5.756. This is the case d = 4 of a conjecture of Hoffman, Johnson and Junge.

This is AI-generated research: the results, proofs and code were found and written by AI. Credit goes to all the humans whose work it builds on.

**Proved in Lean 4:** `FrogModel.transient_four` and `FrogModel.meanVisits_four_le`, with no hypothesis and only the standard axioms; the Lean kernel checks the certificate of Section 7 of the paper, and Comparator with nanoda checks the two theorems against their statements. The data modules are written from the certificate by `code/g3k/gen.sh`, and the constants of the paper by the PARI/GP scripts of `code/gp`.

Assets: the paper, `verification-v1.0.0.tar.gz` (the complete output of every check of the CI run), `frog-model-tree-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz`, the Lake build that Comparator checked, which `lake build :release` and dependent packages download, and `frog-model-tree-certificate.v4.zst`, the certificate (`ASSETS.md`).

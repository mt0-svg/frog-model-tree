The frog model on the rooted 3-ary tree is recurrent, and on the rooted 4-ary tree it is transient: on the 3-ary tree the root is visited infinitely often almost surely; on the 4-ary tree it is visited finitely often almost surely, and the mean number of visits is at most 5.756. These are the cases d = 3 and d = 4 of a conjecture of Hoffman, Johnson and Junge. Version 2 adds the case d = 3 to the paper, the Lean proof and the data; the Lean modules of the case d = 4 are those of v1.0.0.

This is AI-generated research: the results, proofs and code were found and written by AI. Credit goes to all the humans whose work it builds on.

**Proved in Lean 4:** `FrogModel.recurrent_three`, `FrogModel.transient_four` and `FrogModel.meanVisits_four_le`, with no hypothesis and only the standard axioms; the Lean kernel checks by evaluation the certificate for d = 4 and the run, the certificate and the table for d = 3, and Comparator with nanoda checks the three theorems against their statements. The data modules are written by `code/g3k/gen.sh` from the certificate for d = 4 and by `code/m1gen/gen.sh` and `code/d3chain/gen.sh` from the run and the certificate for d = 3; the constants of the paper are printed by the PARI/GP scripts of `code/gp`, and the table of Proposition 15.2 is written by those of `code/dcheck`.

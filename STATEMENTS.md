# From the paper to the proof

Each numbered lemma, proposition and theorem of the paper, and its conjecture, with its Lean declarations (the first, in bold, is the one its proof names) and the scripts and recorded outputs of its checks, or the reason it has none. Generated by `paper/statement_map.sh` from the macros next to each label of `paper/main.tex`. The definitions have no row.

- In the case d = 3 (Sections 8 to 15) the first name of an entry is its proof. For Theorems 8.2 and 9.3 and Lemmas 9.1 and 9.2 it is in `FrogModel/LemmaR/` or `FrogModel/ZeroOne/`; for the others it is in `FrogModel/D3/`, and where the entry has one, the statement it proves follows, a definition of type `Prop` in `FrogModel/D3/Interfaces/`. `FrogModel.D3.Iface.m1run_certS`, Proposition 15.1, is the hypothesis of `FrogModel.D3.Assembly.linear_three'` and `FrogModel.D3.Assembly.recurrent_three_of_m1`; `FrogModel.D3.Chain.m1run_certS_proof` proves it from the data modules that `code/m1gen/gen.sh` and `code/d3chain/gen.sh` write from the two d = 3 assets (`FrogModel/D3/M1Data/` and `FrogModel/D3/CertData/`, not committed), and `FrogModel.recurrent_three` in `FrogModel/SolutionRecurrent.lean` applies the second to it; `code/formal-proof/main_axioms.lean` prints its axioms.
- `FrogModel.transient_four`, `FrogModel.meanVisits_four_le` and the theorems on the data (`FrogModel.checkAll_theTree`, `FrogModel.startOK_theTree`) are in `FrogModel/Solution.lean`, which imports the data modules that `code/g3k/gen.sh` writes from the certificate (`FrogModel/G3Q/`, not committed); `code/formal-proof/main_axioms.lean` prints their axioms.
- Theorems 1.2 and 2.3: `FrogModel.transient_four_of_data`, `FrogModel.meanVisits_four_le_of_data` and `FrogModel.lemmaXFour_of_data` hold for every data tree that passes the checks of Proposition 7.1; `FrogModel/Solution.lean` applies the first two to the data, and `FrogModel.lemmaXFour_of_data` is the step that the second uses.
- Section 6: the declarations are stated for the table of Section 7 (`FrogModel.Cert.cand`), some of them for every table.
